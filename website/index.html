How it works

A short game session teaches the ideas grown-ups wish they had learned early.

Grow your colony

Every cell is worth money. Each round, your saved cells split. Pick slow, medium or fast growth and watch compound growth happen in real time.

Beat the Flash Deal

Pop-up deals tempt you to spend now. Skip them to fill your Resistance Meter and earn bonus cells.

Save for a real goal

Pick a toy, a game or something from the wishlist. See exactly how many cells it costs and how close you are.

Graduate to a new colony

Reach 50 cells and your colony retires to the Colony Museum. Your next colony starts with 3 bonus cells.

What kids learn

Real money ideas, explained with cells, petri dishes and a friendly science buddy.

📈

Compound growth

Saving longer means faster growth. Small amounts snowball over time.

⚖️

Opportunity cost

A cell you spend can't grow anymore. Every purchase has a hidden price.

💪

Impulse control

Flash Deals feel urgent on purpose. Kids practice waiting and get rewarded for it.

🎯

Goal setting

Kids pick something they really want and track their progress toward it.
