# Money Cells — Marketing Plan (Q4 2026 → Q2 2027)

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** *Money Cells turns saving into a growing game — kids ages 6–12 watch their money multiply like cells, and learn why waiting pays off.*

**Analogy we sell with:** a piggy bank just sits there. Money Cells is a *garden*: save and it grows, spend and those cells are gone for good.

| Audience | Role | What they care about | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parents (25–45) | **Buyer** | Screen time that's actually useful, kids who stop begging for stuff | "The game that teaches your kid to say *not yet*." |
| Kids 6–12 | **User** | Fun, cute, progress, rewards | "Grow your cells. Beat the flash deal!" |
| Teachers / homeschoolers | **Multiplier** | Curriculum fit, free, no ads | "Compound growth in 10 minutes, no worksheet." |

**Proof points:** Cellie AI tutor, "Worth it?" photo check, Resistance Meter (impulse control), parent dashboard with AI coaching, no ads, no in-app purchases aimed at kids.

## 2. Ground rules (kids' app)

- **Market to parents, not kids.** No paid ads targeted at under-13s (COPPA, Apple Kids Category, Google Families policy).
- No third-party ad or analytics SDKs that track kids; say "no ads, no tracking" loudly — it's a selling point.
- All creator partnerships feature a parent on camera; kids appear only with parental consent.

## 3. Brand

- New logo: **Cellie** (a smiling green cell) budding a gold coin cell → "your money grows". Files in `assets/brand/`.
- Colors: sky blue `#0284c7`, cell green `#16a34a`, coin gold `#facc15`, action orange `#f97316`.
- Font: Fredoka (rounded, friendly). Tagline: **"Save it. Grow it. Watch it multiply!"**
- Cellie is the face of everything: stickers, the App Store icon, social avatar, and reactions ("Cellie is proud of you!").

## 4. Channels (in priority order)

### A. App Store optimization (free, always on)
- New icon (Cellie on sky blue stands out against dark finance icons).
- Title: *Money Cells: Kids Savings Game*. Subtitle: *Learn money by growing it*.
- Keywords: kids money, allowance, savings game, financial literacy, piggy bank, compound interest for kids.
- Screenshots: 1) Cellie + tagline, 2) cells splitting, 3) Flash Deal "Skip & save", 4) "Worth it?" camera, 5) parent dashboard. Add a 15s preview video.
- Apply for the **Kids Category** (ages 6–8 and 9–11) — boosts parent trust and search placement.
- Prompt for a rating after a colony graduates (the happy moment), parent-gated.

### B. Parent social content (organic TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- 3 posts/week. Formats that work:
  - **"Would your kid skip the deal?"** — parent films kid facing a Flash Deal. Real reactions.
  - **"Cells vs. piggy bank"** — split screen, 30 days later.
  - **Cellie explains** — 20s money idea for parents to watch with their kid.
- Hook every video in the first 2 seconds with the cell-splitting animation.

### C. Parent creators and communities
- Recruit 10–20 micro-creators (10k–100k followers) in mom/dad, homeschool, and personal finance niches. Offer free premium + affiliate/referral code.
- Share (not spam) in r/Parenting, r/personalfinance "teach kids" threads, local parent Facebook groups, PTA newsletters.

### D. Schools and educators
- Free **classroom kit**: a 1-page lesson plan ("Why do cells double?"), printable Cellie colony tracker, parent take-home note with a download QR.
- Target: homeschool co-ops, 3rd–6th grade teachers, after-school programs, Scouts money-management badges.
- Submit to Common Sense Media for a review/rating.

### E. Partnerships
- Credit unions and community banks' youth savings programs (they want young members; we give them a fun tool). Co-branded "Grow your first colony" campaign.
- Libraries' summer reading/learning programs.

### F. PR
- Hooks: "AI tutor teaches kids to resist impulse buys", "Kids' app built by a parent", "The app that makes kids say *no* to toys".
- Pitch parenting outlets (Parents, Motherly, Fatherly), local TV morning shows, and personal-finance podcasts.

### G. Referral loop (in-app)
- "Invite a family, you both get a bonus colony skin" — sent from the **parent** dashboard only.
- Shareable parent card: "Maya saved 50 cells this month 🎉" (first name optional, no photo).
